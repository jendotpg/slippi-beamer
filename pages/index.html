Flash a Slippi Beamer

Hold the button on the side of the dongle down while you plug it into your laptop, then let go. Then:

Flash it in your browser

Connect, pick the port, and flash.

Chrome on desktop only — this uses Web Serial, which Firefox and Safari do not have.

Prefer a terminal? Download beamer.bin and run espflash write-bin 0x0 beamer.bin.
